**Primates**〔[primate](http://data.loterre.fr/ark:/67375/B9M-VTP49WN6-B)〕 is an order of mammals, which is further divided into the strepsirrhines, which include lemurs, galagos, and lorisids; and the haplorhines, which include tarsiers and simians (**monkeys**〔[monkey](http://data.loterre.fr/ark:/67375/B9M-L18SKLTX-D)〕 and apes). **Primates**〔[primate](http://data.loterre.fr/ark:/67375/B9M-VTP49WN6-B)〕 arose 74–63 million years ago first from small terrestrial mammals, which adapted for life in tropical **forests**〔[forest](http://data.loterre.fr/ark:/67375/B9M-K0DK9G4Z-0)〕: many **primate**〔[primate](http://data.loterre.fr/ark:/67375/B9M-VTP49WN6-B)〕 characteristics represent adaptations to the challenging **environment**〔[environment](http://data.loterre.fr/ark:/67375/B9M-V4J5NGKR-M)〕 among tree tops, including large brain sizes, binocular vision, color vision, **vocalizations**〔[vocalization](http://data.loterre.fr/ark:/67375/B9M-HR5D190G-T)〕, shoulder girdles allowing a large degree of **movement**〔[movement](http://data.loterre.fr/ark:/67375/B9M-RM09VTQ2-7)〕 in the **upper limbs**〔[forelimb](http://data.loterre.fr/ark:/67375/B9M-BLLZQWT7-Z)〕, and opposable thumbs (in most but not all) that enable better grasping and dexterity. **Primates**〔[primate](http://data.loterre.fr/ark:/67375/B9M-VTP49WN6-B)〕 range in size from Madame Berthe's mouse lemur, which weighs 30 g (1 oz), to the eastern gorilla, weighing over 200 kg (440 lb). There are 376–524 species of living **primates**〔[primate](http://data.loterre.fr/ark:/67375/B9M-VTP49WN6-B)〕, depending on which classification is used.
